# Frequently Asked Questions

## 1. Which source code management tool/platform can be used for this program?
- You can use any platform that is most convenient for you (external platforms, EPAM platform, cloud-based, etc.).
- The only requirement is to align with your mentor on the choice and provide them with the necessary permissions.

## 2. Is there a base repository for the program? Can my repository be public?
- There is no base repository in this program.
- You are implementing your project from scratch.
- Your repository can be public if you prefer, as it is your codebase.

## 3. Which time management approach can I, as a mentee, use while completing the program?
- There are strict deadlines for each module, and you are required to meet them.
- If you need time for vacation or increased project activities, you must communicate with your mentor in advance and ask them to reflect it in the program calendar.
- You can complete any module faster, share your results with your mentor, and move on to the next module.
- The above conditions apply as long as the general deadline is not missed.
- **Please keep in mind that the training takes place in your personal time, not during working hours.**
- **Please do not expect your mentor to follow your pace.**
- The program coordinator may mark your run as **failed** if you miss **three deadlines in a row** without prior communication with your mentor and proper reflection in the program calendar.

## 4. Can I continue working on the program after the general deadline?
- Yes, the program content will remain available after the general deadline.
- However, your mentor can only assist you **before** your run is marked as failed or **before** the general deadline.

## 5. What are the module assessment criteria? How is the final score determined?
- Your mentor will use a **0-100** scale to assess your results for each module.
- There can be more than one iteration—please discuss in advance with your mentor how many iterations they can assess.
- The criteria for module assessment are listed at the end of each **Home Task** page and are as follows:

  - **1-59** – Functional requirements for both tasks have been met (_ensure you follow Clean Code practices_).
  - **60-89** – Non-functional requirements for both tasks have been met.
  - **90-100** – Written answers to the **‘Self-check questions’**
